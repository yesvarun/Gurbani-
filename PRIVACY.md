# Privacy Policy — Gurbani (Gurbani Live)

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Gurbani is an Android app that listens to Gurbani being recited and shows the matching lines of Sri Guru Granth Sahib with transliteration and translation.

## Summary

The app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. The developer does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information.

## Microphone

The app uses the microphone only while you have tapped the button to start listening, and only to recognise the words being recited so it can find and follow the matching lines.

- The app does not record or save audio, and it does not send audio to the developer.
- Speech is turned into text by the speech recognition service already on your phone (normally Google's). When your phone is online, that service may send audio to Google for processing under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). With an offline speech pack installed, recognition can happen on the phone.
- The recognised words are used on your phone to match scripture lines and are discarded. They are not stored or transmitted by the app.

## Data stored on your phone

The scripture text, translations and fonts are included in the app. The app keeps nothing about you on the phone.

## Internet use

The app works without internet apart from online speech recognition. If the built-in scripture cannot be loaded, the app may fetch scripture text from the public GurbaniNow API (`api.gurbaninow.com`). Such a request contains the search words or page number only, and no information that identifies you.

## Spoken meanings

If you turn on "Spoken meaning", the translation text is read aloud by the text-to-speech engine on your phone. Nothing is sent to the developer.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a new date.
