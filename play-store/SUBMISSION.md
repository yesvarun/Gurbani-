## 2. Store listing — English (default)

**App name** (30 characters max)

```
Gurbani Live: Follow the Paath
```

**Short description** (80 characters max)

```
Recite or listen to Gurbani: it finds the shabad and follows it line by line.
```

**Full description**

```
Gurbani Live listens as Gurbani is recited and shows you where you are in Sri Guru Granth Sahib, line by line.

Start reciting, or hold the phone near the paath or kirtan. The app recognises the words, finds the shabad and keeps the current line in front of you, with its transliteration and English meaning.

WHAT IT DOES
• Finds the shabad from a few recited words
• Follows the recitation and moves to the next line as it is spoken
• Shows Gurmukhi, Roman transliteration and English translation together
• Shows the Ang number for the line on screen
• Finds its place again by itself if the recitation moves elsewhere

THREE MODES
• Self-reading: shows the line you are reading and lights up the words as you say them. It can also speak the meaning of each line in English or Punjabi before moving on.
• Gurdwara: reads one line ahead, so the sangat can see what is coming.
• Kirtan: follows repeated lines and returns to the rahao.

SCRIPTURE ON YOUR PHONE
All 1430 Angs of Sri Guru Granth Sahib are stored inside the app with transliteration and English translation, so the text needs no internet.

LISTENING
Speech recognition uses the Google speech service on your phone. It needs an internet connection, or Google's Punjabi offline speech pack if your phone offers one. The app can take you to the download when the pack is missing.

FREE AND SUBSCRIPTION
• Free: 5 minutes of listening every day.
• Gurbani Unlimited: unlimited listening for ₹100 per month, with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers.
The subscription renews automatically each month until cancelled. You can cancel any time in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions.

PRIVACY
No account, no ads, no tracking. The app does not record or store your voice. Audio is used only to recognise the words being recited.

Scripture text and translations are provided by the GurbaniNow project.

Built with devotion by Varun Duggal.
```

### Data safety **(your call)**

How the app actually handles data, so you can answer truthfully:

- The app has no server, no account, no analytics and no ad SDK. The developer receives nothing.
- Microphone audio is passed to the phone's own speech recognition service (Google). The app does not record, store or send it anywhere itself.
- The recognised words and the daily free-minute counter stay on the phone.
- Purchases are handled by Google Play Billing; the app only learns whether a subscription is active.

Suggested answers on that basis:

| Question | Suggested answer |
|---|---|
| Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? | No |
| Is all user data encrypted in transit? | Not applicable when "No" is chosen |
| Do you provide a way to request data deletion? | Not applicable when "No" is chosen |

If you prefer to be conservative because audio reaches Google's speech service, the alternative is to declare **Audio → Voice or sound recordings**: collected, not shared, processed ephemerally, required for app functionality.
